The operating tradeoffs

Daytime operation

Can align circulation and skimming with swimming and daytime debris. Verify cleaner and sanitizer system requirements.

Nighttime operation

May cost less only if your tariff is lower at night. Check noise impact and whether daytime circulation is still required.

Equal hours, equal kWh

At the same input power, shifting hours alone does not reduce energy use. Ambient temperature alone does not establish savings.

Houston weather

Heat, storms, and cooler months call for observation and retesting. Follow equipment freeze-protection instructions during freezing weather; do not rely on this planner.
